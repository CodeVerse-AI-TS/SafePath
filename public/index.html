        • Guest use stores nothing about you. Routes, reports and hotspots work without an account and are not tied to your identity.

        Our mission

        Know your route.
        Alert in one press.

        Walking alone shouldn’t feel like a gamble. SafePath helps people see which routes have repeated safety reports, choose a safer way to get there, and send a live location alert with a single press when something feels wrong. We’re a student team building it as a free tool, because everyone deserves to move through their city with more information and less worry.

        Safety information should be free

        Anyone can view the map and file a report without creating an account.

        Help should take one press

        In a moment of panic there’s no time for menus, so our panic button needs a single press and nothing else.

        Your privacy comes first

        We store your location only to log an alert, and we never track you.

        An honest note: SafePath is an academic prototype, not a certified emergency service. Always contact local emergency services first. Risk scores are based on community reports, and an area with no reports isn’t guaranteed to be safe.

        Safety limitations

        What SafePath can and can’t do

        We’d rather be upfront than overpromise. SafePath is a student prototype, and these are its limits.

        • Not an emergency service.

          It doesn’t replace the police, an ambulance or any official helpline. In danger, contact local emergency services first.

        • Risk scores come from reports.

          An area with few reports isn’t guaranteed safe, and a busy hotspot may simply be a place where more people report.

        • The map may use sample data.

          In this demo, some hotspots are simulated to show how the system works.

        • The panic button depends on its connection.

          It needs WiFi and a GPS fix, and GPS can fail indoors, so an alert may be delayed or may not send.

        • Routes are suggestions.

          The safer route is a comparison, not a guarantee, so trust your own judgement on the ground.

        • No misuse protection yet.

          We haven’t built checks for false alerts or fake reports.

        Community-first

        Built with the community, for the community

        SafePath is only as useful as the people who use it. Every report adds to the map, and every report helps the next person choose a better route. Viewing the map and sharing a report never requires an account.

        How we ask you to report:

        • Report places and conditions

          A dark stretch, a broken streetlight — not named individuals.

        • Be honest and specific

          False reports make the map less trustworthy.

        • Respect other people’s privacy

          Never share someone else’s personal details.

        Your say: Found a problem or have an idea? Tell us, because this project is shaped by feedback.
